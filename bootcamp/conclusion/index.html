Bootcamp / Epilogue

Conclusion

I hope this Bootcamp served as a good introduction to the Hotwire way of building web apps and hybrid native apps.

There's a lot more we can do with this toolset, but I think this is a good stopping point for the Bootcamp.

If you want to know more about Hotwire, here are some recommended links:

  • The Hotwire Dev Newsletter by Joe Masilotti
  • The Hotwire Example Template Repository has tons and tons of examples written by Sean Doyle

There are other paid courses and books, but I'll only mention the free things here. If you know of other free resources or compilations, please send them my way so I can update it here.

I'd love to hear what you think about this Bootcamp and Turbo Laravel. Feel free to follow me on Twitter or email me to share your thoughts. Go build something amazing!
